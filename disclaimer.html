Disclaimer and how to use this list

Last updated: November 24, 2026

What this site is

Nut Free Fairfax is a personal list. I contact restaurants and bakeries in Fairfax County, the City of Fairfax, and the City of Falls Church, ask about peanuts and tree nuts, and write down what staff told me on that date.

Two kinds of places

The first tab, "100% nut free," lists places whose staff told me their kitchen does not use or store peanuts or tree nuts.

The second tab, "Accommodates nut allergies," lists places that are not nut free. They may use peanuts or tree nuts and may have cross contact. I include them because they told me how they handle allergy orders, such as baking those orders separately or checking ingredient sources. I show what they said in their own words. That is not a promise that their food is free of nuts, and I may not have asked about every allergen. If you need a fully nut free kitchen, use the first tab.

What this site is not

It is not medical advice. It is not a certification, an inspection, or a guarantee that any food is safe for you. I do not visit kitchens, test food, or check ingredients. Staff can be mistaken, and I can only report what I was told. Nothing on this site should be your only source when deciding what is safe for you or your child.

Please always check again

Treat this list as a starting point, not the final answer. You are responsible for contacting the business and doing your own checking before you eat there.

Kitchens, recipes, suppliers, and staff change. Before you eat, call or ask in person, tell them about your allergy, and ask about shared equipment and cross contact. Listings older than 6 months are marked Needs recheck. Even a recent listing can be out of date.

Follow your own doctor's advice about what is safe for you.

In an emergency

If someone is having an allergic reaction, use their prescribed emergency medicine as directed and call 911.

No connection with the places listed

No restaurant or bakery pays to be listed, and being listed is not an endorsement or a guarantee of safety. Places also cannot pay to move up the list.

Corrections and removal

If something is wrong or out of date, or you own a listed business and want a change or removal, email nutfreefairfax@gmail.com. I will review it as soon as I can and update or remove the listing.

Use at your own risk

I work hard to keep this list accurate, but I cannot promise it is complete or correct. You use it at your own risk.
