Before We Order: My 3 Step Nut Allergy Routine

My son orders first.

He tells the server about his peanut and tree nut allergy himself. Clear. Calm. A pro.

I sit there and watch him. And I am proud. Really proud.

Look at him go.

Then it is my turn. I ask a lot of questions. More than most people would. And if the server is not sure, I ask them to check with the chef. Every time.

Why so much? Because a server once told us, with a big smile, "Oh, it's allergy free!"

It wasn't.

I am not telling you this to scare you. It is just why I do things differently now.

We usually eat at sit down restaurants, and I follow three steps every time.

And I know what you are thinking. Three steps just to eat dinner? It sounds like a lot. It is not. The first one happens before we leave the house.

Step 1: Call ahead

The menu online never says much. So I call. I ask what they have for nut allergies.

One call tells me a lot. It also saves us from sitting down at a place that cannot help.

Step 2: Order, then clarify

My son goes first. Then I follow up. I say his exact allergy. I ask if they use nuts or peanuts in the kitchen or the food. I pick the food, and I ask them to make sure there are no peanuts or tree nuts in it.

I do not just point at the menu and hope.

Step 3: If the server is not sure, ask the chef

Every time.

And if the server says "it's allergy free"? I still ask. I still check with the kitchen and the chef.

Instead of "It's probably fine," my rule is this: if nobody can tell me for sure, he does not eat it.

Pilots run the same checklist before every flight, even after thousands of flights. Nobody calls that dramatic.

If I could tell you one thing, it would be this: always check ahead before you dine out.

So that is us at the table. He orders first. I follow up. And if nobody is sure, we do not eat it.

And every time, I watch him say it, and I am proud all over again.

Want a head start? Every place on my list shared details about their kitchen directly with me. Start there, then do your own checking.
